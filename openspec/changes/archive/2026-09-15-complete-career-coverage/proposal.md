## Why

Résumé exploration currently allows editorial selection to happen while extraction is still
incomplete. A long engagement can therefore collapse into one broad story, and the portfolio has no
contract proving what happened to every authorised achievement.

## What Changes

- Inventory and reconcile every substantive résumé source unit before exploration can be complete.
- Preserve distinct achievements and record explicit split, merge, exclusion, and ambiguity decisions.
- Require one accountable portfolio inclusion decision for every authorised achievement.
- Replace the decorative portfolio map with a light-first, interactive career evidence explorer and
  a professional printable résumé view.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `input-resume-explorer`: complete source-unit coverage precedes editorial selection.
- `career-portfolio-output`: inclusion coverage, approved preview, and navigable evidence exploration.

## Impact

Adds small, reusable coverage contracts in Knowledge Enrichment and Publication, strengthens the two
skills and examples, and advances the static portfolio format to a richer offline bundle.
