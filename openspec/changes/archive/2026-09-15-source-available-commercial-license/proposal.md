# Source-available commercial licence

## Why

The owner wants free qualifying personal and non-commercial use while reserving modification,
redistribution, and professional or commercial use. The existing AGPL contract grants broader rights
and therefore no longer matches the intended distribution boundary.

## What changes

- Replace AGPL with PolyForm Strict 1.0.0 and accurately describe the project as source-available.
- Provide separate commercial-licensing guidance and align package, plugin, notice, and trademark files.
- Add a non-personal generated-build marker and an operator-run public GitHub discovery command.
- Keep private use private: no telemetry, tracking, fingerprinting, or phone-home behaviour.

- `plugin-distribution`
