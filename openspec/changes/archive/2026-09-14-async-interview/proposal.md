## Why

Asynchronous interviews can reduce scheduling friction and support richer reflection, but only if
delivery remains structured, accessible, transparent, comparable, and accountable. A chat workflow
without a declared plan risks covert criteria, inconsistent probing, and automated decisions.

## What Changes

- Add `async-interview` with separate prepare, facilitate, and review modes.
- Require opportunity analysis, criteria, questions, anchors, frozen evidence view, policy, notice,
  accommodations, retention, challenge, and accountable human outcome before formal evaluation.
- Preserve delivery events, responses, observations, findings, ratings, and outcomes separately.
- Add an example and update discovery and bundle assertions to eight skills.

Non-goals: casual discovery, covert screening, personality inference, biometric or emotion analysis,
automatic ranking, autonomous hiring outcomes, or application submission.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `async-interview`: Structured and accountable asynchronous interview delivery.

### Modified Capabilities

- `plugin-distribution`: One-command discovery includes eight available skills.

## Impact

The change adds an evaluation workflow skill, delivery record, example, package assertions, and
documentation status changes. It composes the existing interviewer skill and evaluation intent.
