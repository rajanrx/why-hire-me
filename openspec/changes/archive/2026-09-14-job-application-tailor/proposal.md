## Why

A career knowledge graph becomes immediately useful when a person can prepare an application for a
real opportunity. Generic tailoring tools often keyword-match, fabricate alignment, hide gaps, or
lose the relationship between job requirements, career evidence, and generated language.

## What Changes

- Add `job-application-tailor` for URL, PDF, DOCX, text-file, or pasted job descriptions readable by the host.
- Build an atomic, cited opportunity requirement model.
- Map each requirement to a bounded reviewed career view with explicit evidence states.
- Generate a tailored résumé, cover letter, and optional answers with claim-level traceability.
- Keep new statements behind review and stop before submission or employer contact.
- Promote the existing example and update discovery and bundle assertions to nine skills.

Non-goals: application submission, employer contact, universal fit score, applicant ranking, hidden
keyword manipulation, invented experience, or canonical knowledge mutation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `job-application-tailor`: Evidence-traceable preparation for one specific career opportunity.

### Modified Capabilities

- `plugin-distribution`: One-command discovery includes nine available skills.

## Impact

The change adds a coordinating workflow skill, package record, upgraded example, package assertions,
and documentation status changes. It introduces no vendor dependency or external write.
