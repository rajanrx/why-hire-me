# Navigable career portfolio

## Why

The current renderer presents records and a graph, but visitors cannot continuously follow a technology into an engagement, project, outcome, or source. Its oversized presentation also competes with the career information. Résumé length is implicit even though appropriate length depends on career depth and audience.

## What changes

- Make the default portfolio a compact, light, résumé-like career profile.
- Provide Experience, Expertise, Graph, and Evidence lenses over the same authorised records.
- Make every displayed entity a stable deep link with bidirectional contextual navigation.
- Preserve reviewed technology categories so related tools can be browsed together without merging them.
- Make the graph a focused exploration surface with an equivalent relationship list.
- Add an explicit résumé-length preference to preview and projection identity.

## Non-goals

- No new career facts, inference, scoring, visitor analytics, publication, or canonical-storage changes.
- No physical graph-database dependency.

## Capabilities

- `career-portfolio-output`
