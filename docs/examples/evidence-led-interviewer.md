# Evidence-led interviewer example

Use this skill to understand career evidence more deeply or to conduct a declared, structured
evaluation. It will not infer personality, rank a person, or silently turn discovery into a hiring
assessment.

## Prompt

```text
Use why-hire-me:evidence-led-interviewer in knowledge-discovery mode.
Explore the selected project folder to understand my contribution to a difficult migration.
Ask one question at a time and distinguish what the files show from what I tell you.
Do not inspect anything outside that folder.
```

## Expected conversation

```text
Mode: knowledge-discovery
Authorised source: the selected project folder only

The migration plan shows a staged cutover and rollback checkpoints. It does not identify which
decisions were yours.

Question: Which cutover or rollback decision did you personally own, and what evidence could help
confirm that ownership?
```

After the answer, expect a short evidence-linked summary, unresolved questions, and proposed
knowledge for review. A proposal is not trusted career knowledge until the normal admission step.

## Good follow-up prompts

```text
Focus next on the trade-off I made, not the technology list.
```

```text
Show me which statements are direct observations, which came from my answers, and which are still
inferences.
```
