# Standard: Evidence-led interview

## Rule

A formal interview must measure declared, job-relevant criteria through a versioned plan. It must
not improvise a universal judgement of the person.

## Required plan

- opportunity and intended decision use;
- job analysis linking criteria to important work;
- criterion definitions and observable behavioural anchors;
- core questions, permitted probe families, and administration rules;
- authorised evidence-view version;
- response formats and accommodation path;
- scoring, human review, challenge, retention, and monitoring rules; and
- AI model, skill, and prompt/version trace where AI participates.

## Required operation

- Every person receives the same core questions and equivalent opportunity for the same plan.
- Every question and finding traces to criteria and evidence.
- Observation, inference, uncertainty, rating, and decision remain separate records.
- Missing evidence is recorded as insufficient, not automatically negative.
- AI may draft but does not silently adopt a consequential finding or decision.
- Appearance, voice, accent, facial behaviour, confidence, protected characteristics, and proxies
  are not assessment evidence unless a separately approved lawful validation explicitly establishes
  a necessary job-related use.

## Enforcement

Proposed until schema validation, policy checks, and tests prove that formal-evaluation writes
cannot bypass the evaluation plan and human-accountability rules.
